About Dan Pradana

Ideas lead.
AI follows.

For more than ten years, I have worked as a copywriter across industries in APAC, and I continue to do that today. Advertising taught me how to find the idea, shape a point of view, and make every word earn its place.

GenAI opened a different kind of playground. It gave me a way to visualize the stranger ideas that rarely survive mainstream production, then build them into original stories with a strong Southeast Asian pulse. The work can be provocative, culturally specific, challenging, and sometimes intended for adult audiences. The technology is new, but the instinct behind it is still storytelling.

Independent Creative AI Lab

MXVDXN

Building original entertainment IP, powered by AI.

MXVDXN is Dan Pradana's independent creative AI lab, created to research how technology can expand the boundaries of storytelling. It turns raw instincts, cultural observations, and unconventional ideas into worlds that can be seen, tested, and developed.

The focus is not speed for its own sake. It is freedom: sharper cultural texture, bolder genre experiments, and stories that traditional production might ignore, reject, or over-polish. Each title can grow through AI video, animation, live action, comics, games, or whatever format the idea demands.
