My work connects story development, creative direction and hands-on AI production. I build characters, establish the visual language and shape how each episode moves, feels and connects to the wider world.

Behind the worlds
An independent creative playground

MXVDXN

MXVDXN is my personal space for AI experimentation and original microdrama creation. It brings together Southeast Asian street life, Japanese independent cinema, underground culture and an affection for unusual people.

Individual experiments grow into characters, episodes and connected fictional worlds.

The showrunner behind the worlds

Dan Pradana

I’m a Jakarta-based copywriter, creator and showrunner with more than a decade of experience in APAC advertising.

Working across brands, industries and creative teams has shaped how I develop ideas and carry them through production. Through MXVDXN, I bring that experience into independent fiction with a distinctly personal point of view.
